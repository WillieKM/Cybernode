Questions

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. Start with the free security score. For a deeper look, we'll run a free external assessment and walk you through the results on a call.

What does “automated remediation” actually do?

Where it's safe and you've approved it, Cyber-Node can disable compromised accounts, revoke sessions, block malicious IPs, update configurations, open tickets in your tools, and trigger remediation workflows. Anything risky goes to a human for approval first.

Is there a contract?

Plans are billed monthly or annually — annual billing is discounted. We'll agree terms on your demo call.

Do you sign a BAA?

Yes. Business and Managed customers handling PHI can sign a Business Associate Agreement.

What's the Founding Customer Program?

A limited group of early customers who get founding-rate pricing locked for 24 months and direct input on the roadmap. Apply here.
