What the free security score does

  • Looks only at information any internet user can see: public DNS records, your TLS certificate, HTTP security headers, and whether common service ports respond.
  • Does not attempt logins, exploit anything, or send attack traffic.
  • Is rate-limited to prevent abuse.
  • Your email and scan result are sent to our team so we can follow up. We don't sell or share them.

Authorization before testing

We never run intrusive testing (penetration tests, authenticated scans, exploitation) without a signed scope and written authorization from the system owner.

Customer data

  • We collect the minimum data needed to monitor and report on your environment.
  • Cloud and identity integrations use read-only access where possible, and you can revoke it at any time.
  • Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) are available for customers handling PHI.

Customer references & claims
