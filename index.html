The problem

Most security tools hand you 300 alerts. Cyber-Node hands you the few that matter.

Small and mid-sized teams don't have time to triage scanner output. Cyber-Node ranks every finding by exploitability and business impact, explains it in plain English, and tells you whether it can fix it for you.

When the fix is in, it re-scans, updates your score, and keeps the evidence for your auditors, customers and insurer.
